I · the problem

The open kagome star has an exact answer: {{ExactE}}

Triangle bound. \[ H=\sum_{\langle ij\rangle}(X_iX_j+Y_iY_j+Z_iZ_j)=\sum_{t=1}^{6}H_{\tau_t},\qquad H_\tau=2\vec S_\tau^{\,2}-\tfrac92\ \ge\ -3\ \Rightarrow\ E_0\ge-18 \] One singlet per triangle saturates every bound: (c) and (d) are the two ground states.
The 18 bonds split into 6 corner-sharing triangles with no shared bond. A triangle of three spin-1/2 has total spin 1/2 or 3/2, so H_tau is -3 or +3. A singlet on one bond of a triangle forces total spin 1/2 there. The spoke coverings put exactly one singlet in each triangle, reaching -18. They are the only perfect matchings (each tip must pair with a hexagon neighbour, all consistently). ED spectrum: {{ExactE}}, {{ExactEone}}, {{ExactEtwo}}.

III · what noise does

Noise is a bias, not a convergence failure

\[ \operatorname{Tr}\!\big[\mathcal D_p(\rho)H\big]=(1-p)\operatorname{Tr}(\rho H)\quad(\operatorname{Tr}H=0):\qquad {{FloorHvaIdeal:m}}\times{{KeepHvaReadout:m}}\times{{KeepHvaGate:m}}={{KeepHvaProduct:m}}\approx{{FloorHvaFull:m}} \]
Energies at the exact optimum (all-zero parameters), {{NoiseShots}} shots per basis, transpiled to {{CxHva}} CX. Readout alone keeps {{KeepHvaReadout}}, gate plus T1/T2 alone keeps {{KeepHvaGate}}; the product matches the full model. No optimiser can beat this: the variational principle holds for mixed states. It also says a noisy reading below -18 is an artefact. Near-multiplicative structure favours rescaling and extrapolation (M2) and fewer CX (M4).

IV · milestone M0 · honest noisy simulation

The simulator never sampled; fixing it moved the floor by one unit

  • No sampling: Aer's estimator computed exact expectations, so shots and readout error did nothing.
  • Seed reuse: a fixed per-row noise pattern became a fake gradient of about {{SeedBugGrad}} that ADAM climbed ({{PreBestRvbAdam}} → {{PreFinalRvbAdam}}).
  • Biased "best": the trace minimum selects lucky fluctuations; results are now a {{PostFinalShots}}-shot re-measurement.
Code commit 0433fd7. After M0 all four combinations sit at {{PostFinalMin}} to {{PostFinalMax}}. The level shift is the missing readout error; the flat RVB+ADAM line is the fake gradient gone. The seed-bug numbers are quoted from docs/roadmap.md since that code no longer exists. H0 hardware run deferred.

Zero variance at the optimum. \[ \sum_{i}z_iz_{6+i}+\sum_{i}\big(z_iz_{i+1}+z_{i+1}z_{6+i}\big)=-6+\sum_i\big(z_iz_{i+1}-z_{i+1}z_i\big)=-6\quad\text{every shot, every basis} \]
The primitive reports sum |c_i| sigma_i / sqrt(shots), ignoring correlations between terms. Real spread measured at (phi, gamma) = (pi/4, pi/4) without gate noise. At the singlet product, each triangle's two unmatched bonds are perfectly anticorrelated shot by shot, so without noise the sampled energy is exactly -18 with zero variance. With device noise the cancellation breaks: the roadmap measured {{RoadmapSpreadLow}} at 1024 shots at the exact parameters on FakeGuadalupeV2. Consequence: compare methods with repeated runs, not the reported std.

V · milestone M1 · better gradients

Parameter shift is exact for every gate here

eSWAP has generator SWAP/2, HVA rotations PP/2: both eigenvalues +-1/2. HVA shares each parameter over 18 gates, so the rule is applied per occurrence and summed: 72 energies. Noise: forward sqrt2 sigma/eps, central sqrt2 sigma/(2 eps), shift sigma sqrt(K/2); at equal budget B split over m energies, sigma = sigma_B sqrt(m). For RVB shift wins by an order of magnitude; for HVA its 72 energies cancel the advantage.
